# Northstar Analytics — FY2025 Valuation Review

## Financial Performance

FY2025 revenue was **$101.0 million**.

Northstar reported **adjusted EBITDA of $18.6 million** for FY2025.

The company's reported EBITDA after restructuring and stock-based compensation was **$14.2 million**.

> **Note:** Adjusted EBITDA excludes $2.4 million of restructuring expense and $2.0 million of stock-based compensation.

Northstar's enterprise value at the report date was **$650.0 million**.

## Valuation

Any EV / EBITDA analysis should specify whether it uses **adjusted EBITDA** or **reported EBITDA**, because the two definitions produce materially different valuation multiples.
