# Northstar Analytics — Management Outlook

## Historical Performance

Northstar generated **$101.0 million of revenue in FY2025** and **$18.6 million of adjusted EBITDA**.

## Outlook

Management expects FY2026 revenue to be **approximately $128 million**.

FY2026 adjusted EBITDA is expected to be **between $24 million and $26 million**.

Management's longer-term objective is to reach **approximately $150 million of annual revenue within two years**. This figure is an internal target rather than reported historical performance.

## Risk

Management noted that achieving the outlook depends partly on enterprise-customer expansion and successful rollout of the new analytics module.
