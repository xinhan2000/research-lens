# Northstar Analytics — FY2025 Investment Snapshot

## Business

Northstar Analytics provides subscription analytics software to logistics and supply-chain companies. Approximately 72% of FY2025 revenue was recurring.

## Financial Performance

Revenue increased from **$82.0 million in FY2024** to **$101.0 million in FY2025**. FY2025 gross profit was **$62.0 million**, and adjusted EBITDA was **$18.6 million**.

At FY2025 year-end, Northstar held **$30.0 million of cash** and **$125.0 million of total debt**. The company's enterprise value at the report date was **$650.0 million**.

## Risks and Timeline

The three largest customers represented **28% of FY2025 revenue**, creating moderate customer-concentration risk.

Management plans to launch a new enterprise analytics module in **Q2 2026**.
